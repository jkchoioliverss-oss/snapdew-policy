Quick help

Common questions

Select a question to find an answer.

Why can't I see the camera preview?

Find Snapdew in iPhone Settings and make sure camera access is enabled. Depending on your iOS version, it may be under the Apps list.

If another app is using the camera, finish using it and reopen Snapdew. If the problem continues, email us your device model and the message shown on screen.

Why won't my photo or video save?

In iPhone Settings, check that Snapdew is allowed to add photos and videos to your photo library. Also check the available storage on your device.

Using a Premium filter requires an active subscription. Choose a free filter or check your subscription status, then try again.

Does editing change my original photo?

Photos edited from your library are saved as new copies, keeping the originals intact. Check the Photos app to confirm that saving has finished.

I purchased Premium. Why is it still locked?

Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for your purchase. Connect to the internet, then select Restore Purchases on Snapdew's subscription screen.

If Premium remains locked, check that your Snapdew subscription is active in your iPhone's Apple Account subscription settings, then contact us.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Snapdew to manage your subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it. See Apple's cancellation instructions.

For purchases billed by Apple, you can request a refund from Apple. Apple provides information about eligibility and the outcome of your request.

How do AI filter recommendations work?

Before capturing, or while editing a photo from your library, open AI Recommendations in the filter area and request recommendations. Compare three filters and their suggested strengths, then tap a candidate to apply it. You can adjust the strength yourself.

Analysis happens on your device. While waiting, you can check progress or cancel. Recommendations cannot be requested during video recording.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

Snapdew handles capture, filters, photo editing, and AI recommendations on your device. It does not upload your photos to an operator-controlled server for this processing.
